What do these actions do?

Approve

Locks the product against deletion and marks it "Approved". It does not require any compliance package or risk assessment to be complete — it just says this record itself is settled.

Close

For once compliance and risk assessment work is done, e.g. at CE marking. Post-market monitoring can continue on a closed product.

Archive

Hides the product from the portfolio and dashboard without deleting anything. Reversible via Restore.

Delete

A soft delete: hides the product the same way Archive does, but marks it deleted. Nothing is removed from the database, and it can be restored.
