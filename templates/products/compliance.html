The Compliance ratio counts the requirements in force for the product's configurations, according to their current answers. A requirement counts as fulfilled unless its package has an action-required finding for that configuration. Findings belong to a package as a whole, not to single requirements, so one finding keeps all of that package's requirements from counting. A requirement in force for several configurations is counted once. Fix what a finding asks for, update the answers it is based on, and the requirements count again.
