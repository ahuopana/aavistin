# Compare

The **Compare** page (top navigation) shows what differs between two
things of the same kind. Use it to see what a new release changes, how
two hardware revisions differ, or what moved since an assessment was
approved.

You can compare:

- two **configurations** — which hardware and software they combine,
  their markets, the resolved answers and where each came from, which
  packages are in scope, which requirements are in force, the findings,
  and the risk register entries that apply;
- two **assessments** — the same, using the approved snapshot where one
  exists (so *approved* against *now* shows exactly what a stale
  assessment is missing);
- two **software releases**, **software options** or **hardware
  revisions** — their details and the answers set directly on each;
- two **hardware variants** or two **products**, including products from
  different families, if you can see both.

Pick the kind, then A and B, and press Compare. Rows that differ are
marked **changed** or **only in A / only in B**; tick *Show only
differences* to hide the rest. Only things under products where you hold
a role are offered.

Start from a configuration's **Compare…** link on the product page, or
from **Compare with another assessment** on an assessment, to have A
filled in already.
