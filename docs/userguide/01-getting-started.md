# Getting started

Aavistin helps a product manufacturer figure out which regulations apply
to a product — and later, assess and document compliance and risk
against them.

## Signing in

An administrator creates your account (local login, or LDAP if your
organisation has it configured) and grants you a role. Once signed in,
the top navigation gives you:

- **Organisations** — the organisations, product families and products
  you have a role on.
- **Compliance** — a portfolio-wide view of every product you can see,
  with its compliance and risk status.

## Roles

Access is granted per organisation, product family or product, as
**Viewer**, **Editor** or **Approver**. A role granted higher in the
hierarchy (e.g. on an organisation) is inherited by everything below it
(its product families, products and their configurations). Roles are not
tiered — an Editor grant does not imply Viewer, so you may hold either,
both, or neither on a given product.

- **Viewer** can see everything the role covers, but not change it.
- **Editor** can create and edit products, configurations, answers and
  risk register entries.
- **Approver** can approve products, configurations and assessments —
  turning them into frozen, audited records.
